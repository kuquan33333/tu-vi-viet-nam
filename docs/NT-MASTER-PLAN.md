# NT — Nhân Tướng Học AI
Approved scope: NT-00 through NT-11. Production baseline a9e449ebb224acfa108785bf77decdf74b14715f, ui/07-branding, tuvivietnam.pages.dev. Independent branch feature/nhan-tuong-ai. Preserve ui/08-home-preview and production. No PR, workflow dispatch, or deployment until owner approval; Linux QA via Muse instead of Actions quota. ChatGPT alone writes/pushes product code. Muse reads repository, tests exact SHAs, reports TXT, and requests next checkpoint. No changes to existing algorithms or homepage.
## Requirements
- Vietnamese educational cultural atlas, descriptive face geometry only. No inference of personality, morality, health, wealth, longevity, identity or destiny from faces. I Ching casting independent of face; Zi Wei uses separately entered birth data.
- Local opt-in camera/image processing; adult self-use; no uploads, persistent facial data, analytics, logs, or automatic camera start. Stop tracks on exit/background; export without photo by default. Verify licenses and legal obligations.

## Workflow
Every checkpoint: inspect HEAD, scope, code and tests; commit to feature branch only; report full SHA and test commands. Muse tests exact SHA in isolated detached workspace, records exit codes and full logs; cannot edit/push/merge/deploy. Record actual evidence and blockers in NT-PROGRESS.md and NT-AUDIT.md. Do not hide baseline failures or lower gates. Do not advance past a failed required gate without fixing or documenting a truly independent baseline blocker and explicit approval. No production or preview-home modifications. Targets for scan FPS 10–20 and warm image inference <3s are measurement goals, not guarantees. Use current source and license verification when choosing dependencies.
